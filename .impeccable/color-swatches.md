## Color Relationships

### Light Mode Harmony
- **Cool foundation:** Azure blue + warm grays
- **Warm accent:** Copper breaks the cool monotony
- **Tension:** Cool structure + warm moments = memorable contrast

### Dark Mode Harmony
- **Blue-gray surfaces:** Not pure black — personality in depth
- **Luminous copper:** Glows beautifully against deep blue backgrounds
- **OLED-friendly:** Dark enough for power savings, blue-toned for character

## The Copper Moment

**When to reach for copper:**
- ✅ Sale saved successfully
- ✅ Data synced to cloud
- ✅ Cash register balanced (cuadre)
- ✅ Inventory added (nueva entrada)
- ✅ Milestone reached
- ✅ Unit counts and quantities

**When NOT to use copper:**
- ❌ Error states (use danger)
- ❌ Warnings (use warning)
- ❌ Neutral operations (use ink/muted)
- ❌ Navigation structure (use primary)

---

**Delight Thesis:** "Warmth in reliability"  
Copper is the app's signature — use it for moments that deserve to feel human and memorable.
